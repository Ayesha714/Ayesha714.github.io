Research

I am a PhD researcher at the University of Glasgow. My research focuses on rotating magnetohydrodynamics, dynamo theory, and fluid dynamics in rotating spherical shells.

Research interests

  • Rotating spherical-shell dynamos
  • Magnetohydrodynamics
  • Dynamo bistability
  • Force balances in rotating convection
  • Numerical simulations
